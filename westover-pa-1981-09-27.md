## Before We Begin

In Vedic astrology, every planet in your chart sits inside one of 27 star groups called nakshatras. Each one adds a sharper, more specific layer of meaning to whatever planet occupies it. This reading focuses mainly on your Moon's nakshatra, because the Moon shapes your emotional world most directly: how you handle feelings, react to what life brings you, and find your balance.

Your Moon is in Virgo, and its nakshatra is Hasta. Virgo processes feelings carefully, wanting things measured and understood before they're expressed. This deeper layer adds real boldness to that care — a readiness to step in and turn a messy situation into something workable, rather than just observing it. Together, they describe a mind that likes to fully understand something, then take direct, practical action on it.

That thread — care paired with hands-on action — shows up throughout the rest of this reading.

## 5. Rahu & Ketu — Growth, Release & Balance

Rahu and Ketu work differently from the other planets in your chart. They aren't physical bodies — they're two points in space where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's path, the same alignment that causes eclipses. Rahu is your north node, and Ketu is your south node.

In Vedic tradition, Ketu points to natural gifts and abilities you already carry into this life. This is familiar territory, sometimes described as strengths from a past life, available without much effort. Rahu points to what you're actively growing into during this lifetime — less familiar ground, more effort, but real potential. Together, they describe a push and pull that runs through one part of your life. On one side is the comfort of what already feels familiar. On the other is the pull toward what you're still growing into — wherever that axis falls in your chart.

Ketu sits in the part of your chart tied to shared resources, vulnerability, and significant transition. You have an old, familiar comfort with going through deep change alongside another person, without needing to over-explain it. Two other influences reach this same point and soften it: one brings assertive energy, the other brings a wider, more forgiving perspective. Because of that, this natural gift tends to come with real resilience rather than fear.

Rahu sits opposite, in the part of your chart tied to resources, values, and what you say out loud. A second, more forceful energy sits right there too, pushing you to actively assert what you believe rather than staying quiet about it. This is less familiar, more effortful ground for you: learning to speak up plainly about resources and values. It's easier for you to rely on the comfort you already have with deep, quiet change.

The practical balance is this: let your comfort with real transition support you. Don't let it become an excuse to avoid speaking up plainly when something concrete — money, values, a boundary — actually needs to be said out loud.
